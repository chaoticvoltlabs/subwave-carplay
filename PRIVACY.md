# Privacy Policy

**Subwave Road**
Last updated: 2026-08-26

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the
developer or to any third party.

## What the app does with your data

- **Station addresses and credentials.** When you add a station, its address
  and any listener credentials are stored only in this app's local storage
  on your device. They are sent only to the SUB/WAVE station server you
  choose to connect to — never to the developer or anyone else.
- **Song requests.** Text you submit as a song request is sent directly to
  the station server you're connected to, the same way the request reaches
  the station's own DJ software.
- **No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting.** The app does not use
  any third-party SDK, tracking, or analytics service of any kind.
- **No account required.** There is no sign-in, and the app does not create
  or manage any user account.

## CarPlay

The CarPlay screen mirrors the same station list and Now Playing state as
the phone app. No additional data is collected or shared for CarPlay use.
